<h3>Troubleshooting</h3>
<p>You may encounter an issue where Apple cannot verify the application. To override this follow the steps below:</p>
<p>1. Go to System Settings</p>
<p>2. Go to Privacy and Security</p>
<p>3. Scroll down to "Security" and you'll find a notice that "main" was blocked, you can go ahead and click open anyway</p>
